My work sits at the intersection of cybersecurity, digital investigation and practical problem-solving. With an academic foundation in Network Security and Computer Forensics, I am interested in how digital evidence is examined, how threats are understood, and how technical systems can be tested and improved. I value analytical thinking, disciplined methods and clear communication.

01 / MY APPROACH

Investigate.
Build. Explain.

I approach technical work with three priorities: investigate carefully, test assumptions and communicate findings clearly. Whether examining digital artefacts, assessing a security concern or building a tool, I value a methodical process and evidence that can be explained and reproduced.

My areas of interest include digital forensics, Cyber Threat Intelligence, network security, responsible security testing, programming, software development and machine learning. I develop these interests through formal study, independent learning, practical labs and project work.

My background in English-language teaching has strengthened the communication side of my work: structuring information, adapting explanations to different audiences and presenting ideas clearly. These are useful skills for technical documentation, reporting, security awareness and collaboration.

My professional direction is cybersecurity and digital forensics. Software development, quality assurance, IT operations and communication complement that focus by helping me investigate problems, build practical tools and explain outcomes.
